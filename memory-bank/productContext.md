# Product Context: "2127 World Shaper"

## 1. Problem & Exhibition Opportunity
Traditional museum and exhibition exhibits about the future are static (videos, pre-rendered renders, or physical scale models). Visitors cannot see the direct, immediate visual consequences of their personal ideas.

**"2127 World Shaper"** solves this by turning the visitor from a passive spectator into an active "Governor of 2127". By speaking or typing any law, they immediately see the 3D metropolis re-architect itself in real time before their eyes.

## 3. Autonomous Exhibition Mode (Auto-Stream)
When no visitor is actively standing in front of the installation:
- An **Auto-Stream timer** cycles every 14 seconds through a curated queue of diverse 2127 decrees.
- It automatically enters the prompt, animates the progress bar, and morphs the city, creating an ever-changing visual spectacle that draws walking attendees toward the kiosk.
- As soon as a guest touches the screen or speaks into the mic, the auto-stream instantly pauses and gives immediate priority to the visitor.
